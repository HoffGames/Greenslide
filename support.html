How do I play?

Slide the blocks along their own direction: horizontal blocks move left and right, vertical blocks move up and down. Clear a path so the green block can slide out through the gap in the right side of the board.

How do stars work?

Solve a level in the fewest possible moves for three stars, within one and a half times that number for two stars, and in any number of moves for one star. Solving a level unlocks the next one.

Can I undo a move?

Yes. Tap Undo as often as you like, or Restart to try a level from the beginning. There is no penalty for either.

How do I reset my progress?

Open Settings → Reset progress and confirm. This clears all stars and locks every level except the first in each difficulty. It can't be undone.

The colors are hard to tell apart

Turn on Settings → Colorblind mode. It uses high-contrast colors, outlines every block and marks the green block with an arrow.

How do I change the language or turn off sounds?

Both are in Settings. Greenslide is available in English, Spanish, German and French, and can follow your iPhone's language. Sound and haptics can be switched off separately. Sounds also follow your iPhone's silent switch.

Are there ads or in-app purchases?

No. Greenslide is a one-time purchase. Every level is included, with no ads, no in-app purchases and no subscriptions.
