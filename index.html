Greenslide

Free the green block.

Greenslide is a calm sliding block puzzle for iPhone. Slide the blocks along their own direction until the green block can escape through the gap in the board.

225 puzzles across three board sizes: Easy 7×7, Medium 9×9 and Hard 11×11.

One purchase. No ads, no in-app purchases, no subscriptions and no tracking.

For everyone. Colorblind mode, and English, Spanish, German and French built in.
