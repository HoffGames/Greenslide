Greenslide

Privacy Policy

Effective October 14, 2026

In short: Greenslide collects no data at all. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Nothing you do in the game ever leaves your iPhone.

Information we collect

None. Greenslide does not collect, store on any server, share or sell any personal information or usage data. The app does not connect to the internet.

Information stored on your device

To remember your progress, Greenslide saves a small amount of information only on your iPhone:

  • the stars you have earned in each level, and
  • your settings (sound, haptics, colorblind mode and language).

This information is never sent to us or anyone else. You can erase your progress at any time with Settings → Reset progress in the app, and deleting the app removes all of it. If you use iCloud or device backups, this information may be included in your own backup, which is handled by Apple under Apple's privacy policy.

Advertising, analytics and third parties

Greenslide contains no advertising, no analytics or crash-reporting services, and no third-party code that collects data. It does not use Apple's advertising identifier and does not track you across apps or websites.

Purchases

Greenslide is a one-time purchase from the App Store. There are no in-app purchases or subscriptions. Payments are handled entirely by Apple; we never receive your payment details.

Children

Greenslide is suitable for all ages. Because it collects no information from anyone, it collects no information from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
